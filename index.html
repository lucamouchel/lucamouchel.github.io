MIT Work Analytics Lab

US AI Labor Exposure Map

An interactive map of where AI can reshape work across America.

Built with the MIT Work Analytics Lab at CTL. The map quantifies AI exposure across U.S. states, metros, and industries using BLS employment and wage data, task-level job mappings, and current vs. theoretical AI capability scenarios. Under a full-adoption view of current capabilities, AI could perform work equivalent to about 18 million workers and $1.4 trillion in annual U.S. wage-bill exposure — with office, admin, and sales roles among the most exposed.
